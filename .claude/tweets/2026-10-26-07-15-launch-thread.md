Internal tools. Just code.

AwaitIO turns an async TypeScript function into an internal tool. Every await io.… asks a person something in the browser and waits for the answer.

No drag and drop. No hosted service. It runs on your own servers.

🧵

A refund tool, start to finish.

Ask for the order ID, load the order, ask why, save the refund. Each await is a screen your team fills in.

That's the whole tool.

[image] 2026-10-26-07-15-launch-thread-2.png

It's your code, so it uses your Prisma client, your services and your env vars as they are.

Nothing to sync, no API to expose just so a tool builder can reach your data.

-----

Not just forms: pages, tables, stats, folders for navigation, actions linked to each other.
