AwaitIO is source-available under the [AwaitIO License](https://github.com/awaitio/awaitio/blob/main/LICENSE). Whether you pay depends on how many people use your AwaitIO apps.

## Who pays

| Users        | Price                                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------------------------ |
| 1–3          | Free                                       |
| 4–10         | $50 per month                              |
| More than 10 | $250 per month                             |
| Enterprise   | Contract, SLA and custom terms; let's talk |

AwaitIO is free, including commercial use, for up to 3 users, for non-profits and educational institutions, and for evaluation. For-profit organizations with more users need a Company License: one flat monthly price per organization for all its apps and environments.

Users are the real people who use your AwaitIO apps, locally or deployed, however many logins they share.

## How to get a Company License

Sponsor AwaitIO on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/awaitio) with the monthly Company License tier for your number of users. The license is valid while the sponsorship is active.

Every payment comes with an invoice on request. For the invoice, a contract, an SLA or custom terms, write to [p.homoky@gmail.com](mailto:p.homoky@gmail.com).

## Same software for everyone

The free and the paid version are the same software: no paid features, no license keys, no limits and no telemetry. AwaitIO never checks your license; it works on trust.

## Support the project

Don't need a license but like AwaitIO? A one-time or monthly sponsorship of any amount on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/awaitio) funds its development.

See also the [pricing page](https://awaitio.com/pricing).
