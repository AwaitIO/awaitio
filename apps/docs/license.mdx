AwaitIO is source-available under the [AwaitIO License](https://github.com/awaitio/awaitio/blob/main/LICENSE). It is free, including commercial use, for up to 3 users and for non-profits and education. Larger teams pay one flat monthly price.

| Users        | Price                                                      |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–3          | Free                                                       |
| 4–10         | $50 per month                                              |
| 11–50        | $250 per month                                             |
| More than 50 | Enterprise: contract, SLA and priority support; let's talk |

How users are counted, examples for agencies, SaaS and open source, and how to get a license are on the [pricing page](https://awaitio.com/pricing). Questions go to [license@awaitio.com](mailto:license@awaitio.com).
