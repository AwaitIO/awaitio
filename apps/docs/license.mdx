AwaitIO is source-available under the [AwaitIO License](https://github.com/awaitio/awaitio/blob/main/LICENSE). Whether you pay depends on how many people use your AwaitIO apps.

## Who pays

| Users        | Price                                                      |
| ------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1–3          | Free                                                       |
| 4–10         | $50 per month                                              |
| 11–50        | $250 per month                                             |
| More than 50 | Enterprise: contract, SLA and priority support; let's talk |

AwaitIO is free, including commercial use, for up to 3 users and for non-profits and educational institutions. Any organization can evaluate it free for 30 days with any number of users; the 30 days start with its first use and are counted once. For-profit organizations with more users need a Company License: one flat monthly price per organization for all its apps and environments. Both Company License tiers come with standard support. Organizations with more than 50 users agree on an Enterprise agreement, which adds priority support.

Users are the real people who use your AwaitIO apps, locally or deployed, however many logins they share.

Users are counted across your whole organization, including the companies in its group (those it controls, that control it or that share an owner with it), so a group cannot split into small companies to stay free. The 3-user limit applies to individuals, sole traders and freelancers too.

Whoever runs an app pays for its users. When an agency or a freelancer builds an app and the client runs it, the client's users count and the client needs the license; the developers who only build, test or demonstrate it are not users and do not count anywhere. The agency or freelancer may also buy the Company License on the client's behalf, for example as part of its own price; it covers the client in the tier for the client's number of users.

## How to get a Company License

Sponsor AwaitIO on [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/awaitio) with the monthly Company License tier for your number of users. The license is valid while the sponsorship is active.

Every payment comes with an invoice on request. For the invoice, a contract, an SLA or custom terms, write to [p.homoky@gmail.com](mailto:p.homoky@gmail.com).

## Same software for everyone

The free and the paid version are the same software: no paid features, no license keys, no limits and no telemetry. AwaitIO never checks your license; it works on trust.

## What the license does not allow

Without a separate agreement, nobody may sell or license AwaitIO itself or a fork of it, offer AwaitIO or a fork of it as a framework, builder or platform for others to build their own internal tools, or offer a commercial hosted or managed service built on AwaitIO. Building apps with AwaitIO for yourself or your clients is fine, even the same app for many clients, and so is sharing or selling your own components, plugins and extensions for it.

The license is governed by Czech law, with the courts in Prague.
